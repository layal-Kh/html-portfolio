The best Movies According to Me

My top 3 movies of all-time

The Green Book

This is a great movie about an african pianist and and italian american and how their friendship shapes amid racism

A Silent Voice

This is an anime about a boy who bullied a deaf girl till she had to transfer and when he grew up he decided to find her and ask for her forgiveness

Coraline

A gothic dark horror about a girl who finds a secret door to another world identical but better version of hers
